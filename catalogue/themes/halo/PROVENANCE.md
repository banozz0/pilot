# Halo provenance

## Palette

- Pilot's own palette, sampled from a wallpaper in a personal collection (cobalt sky, cream cloud), used as a colour reference only, the way Jungle's was.

Modes: dark only. The terminal colours are picked from that picture; Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived by make_lineup.py's rules, at a near-duplicate score of 14 or more from every other theme (nearest: comet, 18.0) (~/.local/state/designer/rounds/pilot-themes-round2/ (designer round; recipe in tools/, sources in sources.json)).

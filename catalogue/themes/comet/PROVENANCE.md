# Comet provenance

## Palette

- Pilot's own palette, sampled from this pack's wallpaper (a comet mirrored in a still mountain lake), the way Jungle's was.

Modes: dark only. The terminal colours are picked from the wallpaper; Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived by make_lineup.py's rules, with every candidate kept at a near-duplicate score of 14.5 or more from the other themes (Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/themes-20/ in Pilot's project notes).

## Wallpapers

Picked by Sven, 2026-11-25, in round 2 of Pilot's themes, from 2 to 4 candidates for this theme; it replaces the pack's earlier wallpapers. The landscape file is 3840×2160 and keeps its subject inside the centre 21:9 band (what a 3440×1440 screen shows); the tall one is 2160×3840. HEIC at quality 80. Extras picked by Sven, 2026-11-25, from the same round: Salt Flat. The files are numbered so the main one comes first.

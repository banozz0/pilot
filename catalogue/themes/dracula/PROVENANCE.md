## Wallpapers

Picked by Sven, 2026-11-25, in round 2 of Pilot's themes, from 2 to 4 candidates for this theme; it replaces the pack's earlier wallpapers. The landscape file is 3840×2160 and keeps its subject inside the centre 21:9 band (what a 3440×1440 screen shows); the tall one is 2160×3840. HEIC at quality 80. Extras picked by Sven, 2026-11-25, from the same round: Glasshouse. The files are numbered so the main one comes first.

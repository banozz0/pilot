# Isle provenance

## Palette

- Pilot's own palette, sampled from this pack's wallpaper (a cottage on a floating island, moonlit clouds), the way Jungle's was.
- Palette idea: Omarchy's Ethereal (basecamp/omarchy, MIT): night navy with a lavender accent. Ideas only: no file and no colour value of it is copied.

Modes: dark only. The terminal colours are picked from the wallpaper; Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived by make_lineup.py's rules, with every candidate kept at a near-duplicate score of 14.5 or more from the other themes (Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/themes-20/ in Pilot's project notes).

# Mesa provenance

## Palette

- Pilot's own palette, sampled from this pack's wallpaper (a red mesa under the Milky Way), the way Jungle's was.
- Palette idea: Omarchy's Ristretto (basecamp/omarchy, MIT): its terracotta accent was the starting point. Ideas only: no file and no colour value of it is copied.

Modes: dark only. The terminal colours are picked from the wallpaper; Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived by make_lineup.py's rules, with every candidate kept at a near-duplicate score of 14.5 or more from the other themes (Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/themes-20/ in Pilot's project notes).

## Wallpapers

Picked by Sven, 2026-11-13, from a round of 24 (his 12 picks all made art). The landscape file is 3840×2160 and keeps its subject inside the centre 21:9 band (what a 3440×1440 screen shows); the tall one is 2160×3840. HEIC at quality 80.

# Jungle provenance

## Palette

- Sampled from screenshots Sven sent (2026-11-12) of a Hyprland/Waybar rice: dark olive panels, a lime accent, teal
  process names and meter greens, over a misty jungle photograph. The rice's author could not be found (TinEye: no
  match for its btop screenshot; web search: none), so it is credited as found. Colour values only: no file of the
  rice is copied. Red, blue and magenta are Pilot's own picks, as the rice shows none.

Modes: dark only. Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived from the
source by Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/make_lineup.py in Pilot's project notes (`--row jungle`); the terminal
colours are the source's.

## Wallpapers

Picked by Sven, 2026-11-12: the rice's own wallpaper, found at its source at full resolution. The original is
4258×2804; no larger copy exists there. HEIC at quality 80.

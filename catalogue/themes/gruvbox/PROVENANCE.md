# Gruvbox provenance

## Palette

- Omarchy's gruvbox (MIT), which is gruvbox-material dark, medium: github.com/sainnhe/gruvbox-material, MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 sainnhe.
- gruvbox-material light, medium: github.com/sainnhe/gruvbox-material, MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 sainnhe.

Modes: dark, with a light variant (`variants.light`) for apps that follow macOS's mode. Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived from the
source by Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/make_lineup.py in Pilot's project notes; the terminal colours are the source's. Licence texts ship in Pilot.app as Theme-palettes-LICENSES.txt
and Omarchy-LICENSE.txt.

## Wallpapers

Picked by Sven, 2026-11-25, in round 2 of Pilot's themes, from 2 to 4 candidates for this theme; it replaces the pack's earlier wallpapers. The landscape file is 3840×2160 and keeps its subject inside the centre 21:9 band (what a 3440×1440 screen shows); the tall one is 2160×3840. HEIC at quality 80.

### gruvbox-great-wave.heic

- Title: Great Wave
- Author / credit: Katsushika Hokusai, Under the Wave off Kanagawa (The Great Wave), ca. 1830–32; the Metropolitan Museum of Art, H. O. Havemeyer Collection, Bequest of Mrs. H. O. Havemeyer, 1929. Recoloured for Pilot, 2026-11-25.
- Made: a 16:9 crop of the Met scan, greyscale, inverted and mapped to Gruvbox's #282828, #7DAEA3 and #D4BE98, then Lanczos to 3840×2160.
- Source URL: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/45434 (image https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/original/DP130155.jpg)
- Licence: Public domain, The Met Open Access (CC0); the recolour is supplied for unrestricted use with Pilot and exported Pilot theme packs.

### gruvbox-tall.heic

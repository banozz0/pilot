# Gruvbox provenance

## Palette

- Omarchy's gruvbox (MIT), which is gruvbox-material dark, medium: github.com/sainnhe/gruvbox-material, MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 sainnhe.
- gruvbox-material light, medium: github.com/sainnhe/gruvbox-material, MIT, Copyright (c) 2020 sainnhe.

Modes: dark, with a light variant (`variants.light`) for apps that follow macOS's mode. Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived from the
source by Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/make_lineup.py in Pilot's project notes; the terminal colours are the source's. Licence texts ship in Pilot.app as Theme-palettes-LICENSES.txt
and Omarchy-LICENSE.txt.

## Wallpapers

Picked by Sven, 2026-11-25, in round 2 of Pilot's themes, from 2 to 4 candidates for this theme; it comes first, before the pack's earlier wallpapers. The landscape file is 3840×2160 and keeps its subject inside the centre 21:9 band (what a 3440×1440 screen shows); the tall one is 2160×3840. HEIC at quality 80. The earlier wallpapers follow (Sven, 2026-11-25: they stay in themes he kept as is), as they shipped before; their note: Picked by Sven, 2026-11-12. Every landscape file is 3840×2160 and keeps its subject inside the centre 21:9 band (what a 3440×1440 screen shows); the tall one is 2160×3840. HEIC at quality 80. Licences were not audited for this round; each image's source and author are recorded as found.

# Ayu Dark provenance

## Palette

- ayu Dark: github.com/ayu-theme/ayu-colors and vscode-ayu, MIT, Copyright (c) Konstantin Pschera; ANSI as Ghostty's bundled "Ayu".
- ayu Light: github.com/ayu-theme/ayu-colors, MIT, Copyright (c) Konstantin Pschera; ANSI as Ghostty's bundled "Ayu Light".

Modes: dark, with a light variant (`variants.light`) for apps that follow macOS's mode. Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived from the
source by Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/make_lineup.py in Pilot's project notes; the terminal colours are the source's. Licence texts ship in Pilot.app as Theme-palettes-LICENSES.txt
and Omarchy-LICENSE.txt.

## Wallpapers

None ship with Pilot: the app carries this pack's palette only. Its wallpapers are in the catalogue's copy of
the pack, catalogue/themes/ayu-dark/ in banozz0/pilot, which Pilot downloads on Apply; that copy's PROVENANCE.md
records each one.

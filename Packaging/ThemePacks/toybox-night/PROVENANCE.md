# Toybox Night provenance

## Palette

- Pilot's own palette (Sources/PilotCore/PilotTheme.swift); no third-party colours.

## Wallpapers

None ship with Pilot: the app carries this pack's palette only. Its wallpapers are in the catalogue's copy of
the pack, catalogue/themes/toybox-night/ in banozz0/pilot, which Pilot downloads on Apply; that copy's PROVENANCE.md
records each one.

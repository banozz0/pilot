# Akane provenance

## Palette

- Akane by Grenish (github.com/Grenish/omarchy-akane-theme). The repository has no licence: Pilot takes its colour values only and copies none of its files or wallpapers.

Modes: dark only. Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived from the
source by Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/make_lineup.py in Pilot's project notes; the terminal colours are the source's. Licence texts ship in Pilot.app as Theme-palettes-LICENSES.txt
and Omarchy-LICENSE.txt.

## Wallpapers

None ship with Pilot: the app carries this pack's palette only. Its wallpapers are in the catalogue's copy of
the pack, catalogue/themes/akane/ in banozz0/pilot, which Pilot downloads on Apply; that copy's PROVENANCE.md
records each one.

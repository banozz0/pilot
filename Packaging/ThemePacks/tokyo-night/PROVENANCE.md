# Tokyo Night provenance

## Palette

- Omarchy's tokyo-night (github.com/basecamp/omarchy, MIT, Copyright (c) David Heinemeier Hansson), after folke/tokyonight.nvim.
- Tokyo Night Day from folke/tokyonight.nvim (extras/ghostty/tokyonight_day), Apache-2.0, Copyright (c) Folke Lemaitre.

Modes: dark, with a light variant (`variants.light`) for apps that follow macOS's mode. Pilot's own roles (surfaces, lines, second text, module and graph colours) are derived from the
source by Projects/Pilot/design/theme-packs/make_lineup.py in Pilot's project notes; the terminal colours are the source's. Licence texts ship in Pilot.app as Theme-palettes-LICENSES.txt
and Omarchy-LICENSE.txt.

## Wallpapers

Original procedural artwork made for Pilot, 2026-11-11. The source theme's own wallpapers are not used: their rights
are unknown (Sven, Q6). No third-party photos, illustrations, models, downloads or paid requests.

The quiet diagonal field of the other packs (make_packs.py's formula and encoder): the accent at 0.3 over the
theme's ground. Wide and tall are the same field at 3840×2160 and 2160×3840, RGB PNG. No text, marks or embedded metadata.

Rights: original Pilot asset; supplied for unrestricted use, modification and redistribution with Pilot and exported
Pilot theme packs. No attribution or third-party licence obligation.

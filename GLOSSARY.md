# Pilot — domain glossary

Use these words in code, cards and docs. Avoid the alternatives in brackets.

- **Hyper** — the layer modifier: while the layer key is held, every key is a Pilot bind. Presented to users as "Hyper". (not "hyperkey", "meh", "super")
- **Layer key** — a physical key that is Hyper: Caps Lock (default, remapped to F18 at the HID level), left or right Control, left or right Option, or left or right Command. A modifier picked is Hyper alone and no longer itself; its other side still is, so only one side of a modifier can be on (left Command picked, Cmd+C needs right Command). Picked in onboarding; General can turn on more than one, each Hyper alone. (not "trigger key")
- **Chord Hyper** — a real cmd+ctrl+alt chord sent by a keyboard (the Keychron knob macros); counts as Hyper for keys Pilot binds and passes through untouched otherwise.
- **Bind** — one trigger mapped to one action, e.g. Hyper+Shift+B → open browser. Users make binds; they never write config. (not "shortcut", "hotkey" — those words are for macOS's own and other apps')
- **Trigger** — what the user presses: Hyper plus a sublayer and a key, or a plain combo.
- **Sublayer** — the extra modifiers held with Hyper: none, Shift, Ctrl, Alt, Shift+Ctrl. Each is a section of the Binds screen and a page of the overlay. (not "tier" — that word is taken, below)
- **Mouse trigger** — a mouse button, a media key or key a mouse sends, or the wheel, bound on Binds › Mouse and kept in binds.json's `mouse` rows (schema 3). Each button has its plain press and, with Hyper held, a second one (Hyper + Back); the wheel's rolling binds only with Hyper held: Hyper + scroll up, Hyper + scroll down (P51). Button 3, the wheel's press, is the Wheel click (not "Middle", P57) and binds plain and under Hyper like any button. `pilot list mouse` names each by its label, "Mouse 4" or "Hyper+Mouse Scroll Up" (P58). (not "mouse bind" for the trigger itself; the bind is the trigger and its action)
- **Plain combo** — a trigger without Hyper, e.g. left Alt+H or cmd+ctrl+X: at least one of cmd, ctrl, alt, left and right told apart, own section in the Binds screen, warned about when it shadows a macOS shortcut or accent typing.
- **Hold** — a mouse trigger held past Hold after (180 ms, Launcher › Rings), a trigger of its own: "Mouse 4 Hold", in binds.json's optional `holds` list (P74). Its press waits; held longer its bind runs (a ring), let go sooner it is the button's click: its own bind, or with none a media key goes on to macOS's Now Playing, and a button's or a keystroke's does nothing (Sven, Q370). Never under Hyper. (not "long press")
- **Ring** — the Ring menu (P74, after Opie; design Halo): up to eight wedges around the pointer, opened by holding a key or a button's hold bound to `rings.open:<ring>`; flick toward a wedge and let go, or click it. A group nests as the menu's branches do: entering it turns the ring into the group's own ring in the same place, a rest in the hub or Esc going back up a level (crossing the hub never does); past eight, More pages. The Pilot ring is the Hyper+Space menu's root as Sven arranged it, its extra rows too, each branch filled as it opens. Edited, and given a key or a button's hold, in Launcher › Rings. Rings live in rings.json (schema 2); a ring for one app opens first while it is in front; scroll at the top steps to the next ring. (not "pie menu", "radial", "wheel" — the wheel is the mouse's)
- **Wedge** — one of a ring's eight places, north first, clockwise: an action, a switch (an action showing ON or OFF, read as the ring opens) or a group, written by hand or filled from a menu branch, entered as its own ring, groups inside groups. (not "slot", "segment")
- **Action** — one thing Pilot can do: snap left, go to workspace 3, open terminal, lock. Every action is bindable; each has an id, a label, a default key and a tier. (not "command")
- **Ability** — anything Pilot can do as Launcher › Abilities lists it (story 155): every action, plus what the Hyper+Space menu answers when typed (the calculator, file search, Ask AI), which no action names. (not "feature")
- **Module** — a group of actions with one mechanism: Engine, Windows, Workspaces, Apps, Drive, Captures, System, Media, Dictation, Rings (`Module` in PilotCore's ActionRegistry); later the Game.
- **App shortcut** — an app's own menu item with the key set over it on Binds › Per app › App shortcuts (story 151): an app bind that presses the item, or one that turns the app's own shortcut off. (not "app bind" in general: an app bind can run any action) Parked 2026-10-10 with per-app binds (branch parked/per-app-binds); binds.json keeps them, unused.
- **Preset** — a named bind set, picked on the Binds screen: a base table (Pilot default, My binds, or empty for Start from scratch) plus the keys set in it, which stay with it. Pilot default and My binds are built in, My binds listed only for a binds.json that already holds it (SH8), so a new user sees Pilot default alone; the rest are made by hand or imported, and can be renamed and deleted. App binds (parked 2026-10-10, kept in binds.json) stay across every preset; Reset all goes back to the active preset's base. (not "pack" — a pack is exported binds)
- **Default bind set** — Pilot default, the preset a new install starts on (story 149): since 1.0 Sven's own layout, his Arrows 2 keys and mouse buttons frozen in `PilotDefault` (Q531, 2026-10-28), Hyper+Shift+G opening github.com rather than his GitHub web app (Q542). Before 2026-10-09 it meant the Omarchy preset: Omarchy's layout on Hyper, taken from the Mac column of Sven's shared keymap (`~/code/dotfiles/keymap/keymap.toml`). Resettable from the Binds screen. (not "starter pack" — the pack picker is v2)
- **Interim layout** — Sven's own keys as they were on 2026-10-04: the My binds preset, selectable on a Mac whose binds.json already holds it, never offered to a new user.
- **Default apps** — what the Open with page sets (story 163): the main editor and the app macOS opens each kind of file and link with, changed only on a pick. (not "file associations", not "Defaults", the page's name until C3)
- **Main editor** — the Editor role's app: Sven's pick on Open with, else the Mac's default for text when it is an editor, else VS Code, Cursor, Zed or Sublime Text, else TextEdit. Its key opens it and Configs open files in it. (not "default editor", which is macOS's per file kind)
- **Configs** — the app window page, and the menu's Config branch (a More rows extra, off by default), listing the config files of the apps on this Mac, read-only, each opened in the main editor; its Agents tab lists the agents' settings (Claude Code, Codex, Hermes, Empryo, opencode; on Configs itself until 2026-10-19) and the files agents read before they work (the Agent instructions page until the page map, Sven 2026-10-17). (not "dotfiles", which is Sven's repo the files often live in)
- **Menu** — the searchable, hierarchical panel Hyper+Space opens, set up on the Launcher page. Branches: Apps, Learn, Trigger, Install, Remove, Update, Clean, Extensions, About (this Mac and Pilot, Omarchy's About, Q443), System; the root shows only these, in Sven's order minus the ones he hid (Launcher › Your menu, story 152), with any extra rows he switched on (More rows: Config, which left the default root on Sven 2026-10-21, Files, Notes, QR, Stats, …) after System until he moves them among the branches, no recents or pins over them (Sven 2026-10-20), and typing there searches them all, hidden ones too, plus windows and files. Drawn as Line (the default, story 193), Classic or Rail, picked on Look (story 74). (not "launcher", "palette", "cheat sheet")
- **Launcher** — the app window's page for the menu, in an area of its own after Keys (P37, Sven 2026-10-18, Q284, Q285): Your menu (its root rows, Translate), Abilities (each with its own settings under it: repos, GIFs, passwords), Aliases and Quicklinks. The Menu page until the page map (Sven 2026-10-17, Q211), renamed Launcher on 2026-10-18; its links still say `page=menu`. The panel itself stays the menu. (not "Menu page")
- **Classic** — the menu layout Omarchy's looks like, bigger, drawn as a terminal pane since Q139 (Classic Shell, story 193): JetBrains Mono, an accent border, a ❯ prompt line, recents and branches under rule headings at the root, ledger rows (key on the left) in lists, a cursorline on the current row and a status line at the foot; the default from 2026-10-15 until Line (story 74). Line draws its root. (not "Instrument", the style it draws in; not "list mode")
- **Status line** — Classic's foot since Q139: the mode in an accent block (GO, SEARCH, CALCULATOR…), a root search's kinds with their counts, and the keys; it replaced the kinds line (story 193). (not "foot", the plain hint line Instrument and Line draw; not "kinds line", gone)
- **Cursorline** — Classic's current row since Q139: a flat full-width band with a 3 pt accent bar in the gutter, as a terminal editor marks its line (story 193). (not "pill" or "highlight")
- **Line** — the default menu layout since 2026-10-16: it opens as Classic's narrow list and the first key grows it into a 750 search whose line completes the best match and shows an answer's result; ⌘1–9 run rows, Cmd+K steps into the row, the kinds sit in the foot (story 193). (not "compact mode", Raycast's; not "Spotlight mode")
- **Search the web** — the row every root search offers under its best match (first when nothing matches), beside Ask AI: Return searches what was typed in the default browser with that browser's own default search engine (story 193). (not "web fallback", not "Google it")
- **Morph** — Line growing from Classic's root into its search on the first key, and back when the query empties: 200 ms out, 160 ms back, the search field never rebuilt (story 193). (not "transition", SwiftUI's word for one view's way in or out)
- **Rail** — the menu layout with the kinds on a rail beside the list, a count of hits on each, and Go to (the root branches and Clipboard ↗) under them; an icon strip inside a branch (story 74). (not "sidebar", the app window's; not "Strip", a scrolling workspace's columns)
- **Answer card** — the first answer row of the menu (a sum, money, a time zone, a date, a colour, a translation) drawn big: what was typed on the left, the result on the right, a typed hex's swatch, ramp and five spellings; the row stays what Return copies, with Ask AI under it (story 74). (not "result card", "preview")
- **Line chip** — a picker at the right of the menu's search line that opens its list over the menu's top right: Emoji's category (⌘P), Translate's From, swap and To (Tab steps them, P40); the list takes the keys while open. (not "dropdown", "popover")
- **Speed test view** — Line's view for Test internet speed (P41): download and upload gauges, responsiveness and the last runs, filled live from networkQuality; it stays until Escape, and a run carries on behind it. (not "Speedtest", Ookla's, which Pilot does not use)
- **Stats cards** — the five readouts a root search for stats, cpu, memory, ram or disk shows above the rows in Line (P42); never a row, never the selection. (not "answer card", which is a row)
- **Clean** — the menu branch that shows Mole's preview of what it would remove and runs `mo clean` only after the confirm step, in the visible terminal (story 117). (not "optimize", "purge" — Mole's other commands, which Pilot does not run)
- **Report** — the record Pilot keeps of one Update or Clean run (story 153): when, the exact command, what it was asked to change, how it ended (done, failed with its exit code, cancelled) and what it printed; listed on Activity › Reports, the newest 50 kept. (not "log", which is the system log; not "history")
- **Login and background items** — what starts at login or runs in the background, as Mac › Update & Clean lists it (Q386): Open at Login's apps (login items), and the launchd jobs in ~/Library/LaunchAgents (your agents, the only ones Pilot turns off), /Library/LaunchAgents and /Library/LaunchDaemons. (not "Launch at login", which is Pilot's own setting on General; not "startup items")
- **Branch** — one level of the menu, e.g. Trigger → Capture. Escape goes up one branch.
- **Frecency** — the ranking score: how often and how recently an entry was picked. Kept on the Mac.
- **Alias** — a short word on a Hyper+Space row (`wp`); typed exactly at the root search it lists that row first, a query only starting with it ranks as before. Kept by the row's frecency key in `aliases.json`.
- **File index** — Pilot's own index of the home folder that the menu's file search reads: a helper in the bundle built from Cardinal's MIT engine, kept current through FSEvents. Spotlight is not used. (not "Spotlight search")
- **Text index** — Pilot's own on-disk index (SQLite FTS5, `image-text.sqlite`) of the words Vision read in the images of the folders Text in images names, searched by the Files view for text hits (story 77). Read in a `pilot-text` child, never at search time, and kept apart from the file index. Switching Text in images off or Clear text index deletes it. (not "OCR cache", "Spotlight")
- **Downloaded from** — where a file came from, shown on its row as "from github.com": the site a browser saved it from (`kMDItemWhereFroms`), else the app that saved it (the quarantine's app name, "WhatsApp"); typed `from:<word>` filters on it (story 77). (not "origin", "source app")
- **Duplicates** — Files › Duplicates: groups of files with identical contents (SHA-256), found only when Scan is pressed, each keeping one copy while Move to Trash… sends the rest to the Trash after one ask (story 176). (not "look-alike photos", which are similar, not identical, and not in v1; not "clean up")
- **Folder design** — artwork Pilot sets as a folder's icon (Files › Folders, or Dress selected folders on Finder's selection in Hyper+Space): one of twelve designs Pilot draws, one of Your designs, or a picture of your own (story 196). (not "folder colour", "Customize Folder", which is macOS 26's own colour, symbol or emoji, never written by Pilot; not "theme")
- **Your designs** — pictures added to Pilot's own folder (Files › Folders › Add designs…) to dress folders in, kept as copies, each removable (story 196, P55). (not "Your own", the one picture picked each time; not "pack")
- **Dressed** — a folder wearing a folder design Pilot set and stamped; Reset takes back only a dressed folder's icon, putting back what it had before (story 196). (not "customised", which any custom icon is)
- **File actions** — the picker Hyper + right-click on a file opens at the pointer (story 199): Pilot's own actions for the files under it in Finder, on the desktop or in the menu's Files view, filtered by their kind (`FileAction`); a file row's Cmd+K in the menu stays its own list. (not "context menu", which is the app's own right-click menu, nor "Services", Finder's Convert/Resize/Upscale with Pilot)
- **Desktop menu** — the native menu Hyper + right-click on the empty desktop opens at the pointer (Q743): Change wallpaper… (the wallpaper gallery), Wallpaper settings…, Add widget, New note, Show desktop, Look's style and theme and Desktop icons (Finder's own, off and on), each what the rest of Pilot runs (`DesktopMenu`); over a file it is File actions, over a widget the widget's own menu.
- **Show preview** — File actions' window showing one file's contents on Look's glass: Markdown rendered, text and code, a picture, a PDF's pages, else QuickLook's view; Escape closes it (story 199). (not "Quick Look", macOS's own panel, which File actions also offer)
- **Window search** — typing a window's title or app in the menu and jumping to it on any workspace.
- **Learn → Keybindings** — the menu branch listing every bind, the essentials first in Omarchy's order, searched, running one on Enter; what Hyper+K opens. Replaces the old "Hyper+K sheet". Distinct from the overlay.
- **Learn** — the menu branch that teaches keys: Keybindings only. Its config branches (This app, Shell, Ghostty, Git, Neovim, tmux, read live and read-only, story 78) have their parsers built and wait, parked, until after launch (Q132). (not "help", "cheat sheet")
- **Overlay** — the panel shown while Hyper is held 0.4 s: that sublayer's binds, all, most used or the user's pick. Up to five columns, then "+ N more"; its foot's Edit what shows opens Binds › Overlay.
- **Binds › Overlay** — the tab that picks what the overlay shows: every Hyper bind of the active preset with a Show switch. The pick is one set of actions for every preset; the first switch turns the overlay to My own pick.
- **Swap keys** — Binds › Swap keys: pairs of keys swapped at the HID level while Pilot runs, each typing the other (Caps Lock ↔ Left Control), written with Caps Lock's F18 as one UserKeyMapping set and taken off at quit (Q406). A Hyper key cannot be in one (Q407). (not "remap", which is another tool's mapping)
- **Pilot's tiler** — the tiling engine inside Pilot (`PilotTiler`), ported from AeroSpace (MIT); the only tiler since 2026-10-10 (ADR 0011). Changed 2026-10-10 (R3): AeroSpace, the separate app Pilot drove through its CLI, is gone; its `~/.aerospace.toml` is only read once, as an import.
- **Layout preset** — how a workspace's tiles are arranged: main + stack, columns, rows, grid, scrolling or stage, cycled with one key; a workspace set to auto is laid out again when a window opens or closes. (not "layout mode", "tiling mode") Changed 2026-10-12 (ADR 0014): scrolling replaced accordion. Changed 2026-10-30 (ADR 0015): stage added.
- **Scrolling layout** — a workspace root layout where each tiled window is a column on a strip that scrolls sideways (story 170, ADR 0014); replaced accordion on 2026-10-12. (not "accordion", "carousel", "niri mode")
- **Stage layout** — a workspace root layout that tiles the focused window's group in the centre (a joined group, or a window with its app's other lone windows) and shelves the rest in the hide corner, keeping 232 pt free at the monitor's left edge for the Window shelf (ADR 0015). (not "Stage Manager", macOS's own)
- **Window shelf** — the panel in that room on a Stage workspace: this workspace's shelved windows and groups and the windows minimized from it, the other workspaces folded below; a click focuses one, which brings its group to the stage, or restores a minimized one onto it (ADR 0015). "Shelf" in the UI. (not "Strip", a scrolling workspace's columns; not "sidebar", "rail", "tray")
- **Window pictures** — how the Window shelf draws a window: Stills (the default: a still taken as a window leaves the centre, refreshed every 2 s while the pointer is on the shelf), Live (a capture running per shelved window) or Off (its app's icon and title); pictures need Screen Recording, and until it is allowed the shelf shows icons and offers it once (ADR 0012, amended 2026-10-30 and 2026-10-31). (not "thumbnails", which are the capture's floating card; not "previews")
- **Column** — one place on a strip: a window, or windows joined into it as tiles, at its own width fraction of the usable width (1/3, 1/2, 2/3, full by default). (not "tile", "pane"; a tile is a slot in a tiles layout)
- **Strip** — the row of columns of one scrolling workspace, on the monitor that workspace sits on; it scrolls the minimum that brings the focused column into view, or centres it as the centre mode says (never, on overflow, always). (not "canvas", "tape"; not the strip block an extension sends) Changed 2026-10-20: centre modes.
- **Column rule** — an app's own column on a strip: a width its windows' columns keep through every refit, and whether a window it opens takes focus (`pilot list column-rules`). (not "app rule", which sends an app's windows to a workspace)
- **Sliver** — the few pixels (5 by default) a column past an open screen edge keeps on screen; a click on it focuses that window. A column that would cross onto another display hides in the hide corner instead, never as a sliver. (not "peek", "edge")
- **Workspace preset** — a named setup for one workspace: its apps in order, laid out as a layout preset or at a size per app; switching to the workspace opens what is missing and pulls running windows in, never a duplicate. (not "profile", "scene")
- **Pilot block** — Pilot's tiling settings (app rules, always-float list, gaps, the top bar's room), kept in Pilot's own settings and handed to Pilot's tiler. Named for the marked block it once wrote into `~/.aerospace.toml`; changed 2026-10-10 (R3): that file is read once as an import, never written.
- **Always-float list** — the apps whose windows Pilot's tiler never tiles, kept in the Pilot block by bundle id (C8, 2026-10-10); a name from before that no app answers to is kept and matched by name.
- **Workspace** — a virtual workspace of Pilot's tiler, numbered from 1, nine by default and between 1 and 9 as the Workspaces page adds and removes them (story 62), each opening on its pinned monitor (story 148); windows on hidden workspaces are parked off-screen. Replaces "desktop" as the user-facing word. (not "desktop", "space" — a macOS Space is something else, and Pilot does not manage Spaces)
- **Tiled / floating** — a tiled window is placed by Pilot's tiler; a floating window is placed by Pilot (halves, thirds, ¾, centre) through Accessibility.
- **Pinned window** — a window Pop out (Hyper+Alt+O) floated, centred and pinned: carried to the shown workspace of its monitor on every switch and raised, never parked; kept by Pilot's tiler only, so a restart drops it (story 180). (not "sticky", "always on top": it is raised, not kept above)
- **App rule** — an app pinned to a workspace (Ghostty → 1), kept in the Pilot block so the tiler places the window as it appears.
- **Snapshot** — the one saved record per workspace of its windows: app, title, order, tiled width or floating frame, and layout; Hyper+Ctrl+S saves it, Hyper+Ctrl+Shift+S restores it, reopening closed apps. Changed 2026-10-04: was per desktop and display.
- **Rescue** — the action that pulls windows stranded off-screen on hidden workspaces back onto the main screen, run by its bind while no tiler runs and quietly at Pilot's launch (after a crash). Changed 2026-10-10 (R3): no prompt on AeroSpace's quit, no "always" tick.
- **Drive action** — an action that runs another app's command: its URL scheme, its menu item through Accessibility, a keystroke into the focused app, or the advanced field (URL, Shortcuts name, shell command). Never a keystroke aimed at another app's global hotkey (ADR 0001).
- **Menu item row** — a root search row for one menu item of the app Sven was in, read through Accessibility as the menu opens; Return presses it by its drive route, `drive.menu:<bundle id>:<path>` (story 132).
- **Drive catalogue** — the shipped JSON of known apps and their plain-English drive actions, each checked against the vendor's docs. An app with no route is "not supported". Each action is also its app's command in the menu (P38). (was "Catalogue")
- **App command** — one of an app's own commands the drive catalogue holds, listed at the menu's root search as a Command row under the app's icon ("Calendar · Command") and run by its route, never a key sent to the app (P38, Sven Q289). (not "app shortcut", parked; not "menu item row", read live from the app in front)
- **Setting row** — a root search row for a page or part of Pilot's window (`window.page:<name>`) or a System Settings pane (`system.pane:<id>`), found only by typing; the window's search lists the panes too (story 177).
- **Quicklink** — a named URL with a keyword, `{query}` in it filled with the words typed after the keyword in Hyper+Space (`gh pilot`); opens in the default browser, binds as `links.open:<id>`.
- **Script command** — an executable file in Pilot's Scripts folder, listed in Hyper+Space and bindable as `scripts.run:<file name>`; its Raycast-style header names its title, emoji icon and mode (silent, HUD line, visible terminal), and up to three arguments, typed after its title in Hyper+Space and passed as $1 $2 $3. It runs through the drive shell route.
- **Extension** — a folder in Pilot's Extensions folder (`~/Library/Application Support/Pilot/Extensions/`) holding `extension.json`: an id, a title, an icon, the permissions it declares (shown, not enforced) and its commands, each a program speaking JSON lines that Pilot runs and whose views Pilot draws (story 141, ADR 0010). (not "plugin", "add-on"; Raycast's extensions are not run)
- **Extensions folder** — `~/Library/Application Support/Pilot/Extensions/`, one folder per extension, read again each time the menu opens; Open Extensions folder shows it. (not "plugins folder"; the Scripts folder holds script commands)
- **Catalogue clone** — the person's clone of the catalogue's repo (banozz0/pilot-extensions), set in `extensions.catalogueClone`, that `pilot ext publish` (the /publish skill, P49) sends an extension from as a pull request; a debug build only until v1.1 (SH19). (not "the catalogue", which is what Pilot installs from)
- **Extension command** — one command of an extension: a Hyper+Space row bindable as `ext.<id>.<command>`, no-view (a HUD line) or a view Pilot draws. (not "script command", which is a single file in the Scripts folder)
- **Extension session** — a list command marked `"session": true` whose one process stays running while its view is open, sent each search as a query message and stopped as the view closes (story 141, ADR 0010). (not "daemon" or "server"; a one-shot command runs again per search)
- **Extension preference** — a setting an extension declares (text, password, checkbox, dropdown, app, file or folder), its value kept by Pilot in the extension's ExtensionData folder or, for a password, the Keychain, and handed to every run in the run message (story 141, ADR 0010). (not "config" or "env var"; never passed in the environment)
- **Extensions page** — the app window's page: one list, the installed extensions then the extension catalogue's others (Q601), filtered All, Installed or Updates, the picked one's detail beside it: an installed one's settings, keys and what it touches, with the folders that failed and their reason (story 141); a catalogue one's Get, what you get and what it will touch (story 156, Q600). (not "extension store" or "plugin manager")
- **Extension detail** — a view Pilot draws for an extension in place of a list: markdown with facts beside it and actions under Cmd+K (story 141). (not "preview" or "page")
- **Hidden command** — an extension command marked `"hidden": true`: no Hyper+Space row, no bind, not listed on Binds or the Extensions page; only its own extension reaches it, by a `run` or `push` action or a timer's end (ADR 0010). (not "private command")
- **Extension timer** — Pilot's one countdown, started, held or stopped by an extension's `timer` action and shown with its title in the Timer bar item and the dropdown's Now block, with the extension's buttons; a new one replaces the one running, at zero its end runs as that extension's, and a restart drops it (story 141, ADR 0010). (not "pomodoro", which is the extension that uses it)
- **Info HUD** — Pilot's own HUD that Hyper+Ctrl+Alt+T flashes for 4 s on the focused window's screen: the time, the date, and the battery, the weather and the Wi-Fi network where this Mac has them, read on the press (story 188). (not "notification", which is macOS's; not "level HUD", which shows a screen's brightness)
- **Extension alert** — Pilot's own HUD panel an extension's `alert` action shows: a heading, a note, a sound and up to three buttons (OK when none), staying until one is pressed, which closes it and runs its action as the extension's (story 141, ADR 0010). (not "notification", which is macOS's)
- **Extension purpose** — what an extension is for, in plain words (`description`), and one or two concrete uses (`examples`), from its manifest or the catalogue's index; shown in a catalogue entry's detail, as an installed row's one line, and on its Extensions row (story 158). (not "tagline", "summary")
- **Uninstall** — an extension's folder moved to the Trash from its Installed card, its catalogue card or the menu's catalogue, after a confirm on the page; its ExtensionData and Keychain items stay, so installing it again picks up its settings (story 158). (not "delete"; "Remove" was the catalogue's old word)
- **Extension catalogue** — the extensions Pilot offers from its own GitHub repo, banozz0/pilot-extensions (its `index.json`): the Extensions page's Catalogue tab and the menu's Extension catalogue, read from GitHub only while one is open; each installs, updates or goes to the Trash, its ExtensionData kept (story 156). Pomodoro lives there. (not "store", "marketplace")
- **Extension surface** — where an extension shows beside its Hyper+Space branch: its page in the app window or its bar item, each one of its commands named in the manifest's `surfaces`, asked again on its pace and answered with blocks (story 161, ADR 0010). (not "widget", "panel")
- **Extension page** — an extension's page in the app window, listed under Extensions in the sidebar, drawn by Pilot from its blocks and asked again only while it shows (story 161). (not "Extensions page", which lists them all; not "extension detail", which is in the menu)
- **Block** — one part of an extension surface's answer: stats, a meter, a list, markdown or buttons, and since Surfaces v2 a section, a chat or an input, and since 2026-10-15 a hero or a strip block, laid out by Pilot in the style picked (stories 161, 165, 184). (not "component", "widget")
- **Hero** — a block that leads with a big now: a glyph, a value and its unit, a place, a tone and up to three small stats; Weather's current reading (story 184, ADR 0010). (not "header", "banner")
